🌿 About Surabhi Riverline Honnavar Boating

Surabhi Backwater Tours offers a unique and immersive experience of the beautiful backwaters of Honnavar. Our experienced guides will take you through serene canals, lush coconut groves, and picturesque villages to give you a true taste of local culture and nature. We prioritize your safety and comfort, ensuring a memorable adventure.

✅ Safety First

  • Life Jackets: Every passenger is provided with a life jacket before boarding. Wearing it is mandatory.
  • Boat Condition: Boats are regularly inspected and maintained.
  • Weather Advisory: Boating is paused during heavy monsoon rains or unsafe wind conditions.
  • Boarding Points: Most boarding jetties are secure and managed by local staff.
